AI Money Test

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

What this app is

AI Money Test is a private publishing tool run by the creator of the AI Money Test channel. It uploads short videos that the creator made to the creator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Information the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens that each platform issues when the creator authorizes the app. They are used only to upload and publish the creator's own videos and to check that an upload finished.
  • Basic account details returned with those tokens, such as the account ID and display name, used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The videos, titles and captions the creator chooses to publish.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, contacts or viewing history, and it does not collect information about viewers of the videos.

Where it is stored

Tokens and videos stay on the creator's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than the platform the video is being posted to.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The creator can delete the saved tokens at any time, or remove the app's access from the security or connected-apps settings of each platform. Once removed, the app can no longer post.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
